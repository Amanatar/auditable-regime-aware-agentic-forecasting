# Time-MoE: Billion-Scale Time Series Foundation Models with Mixture of Experts

- **Authors:** Xiaoming Shi et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2024; arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16040
- **Method summary:** Trains a sparse mixture-of-experts TSFM with conditional computation.
- **Datasets:** Large multi-domain pretraining collection and public benchmarks.
- **Key reported result:** Reports strong scaling and transfer while activating only a subset of experts.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Scale and training cost make independent reproduction difficult; finance-specific evaluation is not central.
- **Sub-topic:** probabilistic TSFM
