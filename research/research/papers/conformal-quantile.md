# Conformalized Quantile Regression

- **Authors:** Yaniv Romano et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2019; NeurIPS / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03222
- **Method summary:** Calibrates conditional quantile models with conformal residual corrections for finite-sample coverage.
- **Datasets:** Synthetic and benchmark regression datasets.
- **Key reported result:** Provides marginal coverage guarantees with adaptive interval widths.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Coverage is marginal/exchangeability-based and can fail under temporal dependence or shift without adaptation.
- **Sub-topic:** uncertainty calibration
