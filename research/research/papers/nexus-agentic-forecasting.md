# Nexus: An Agentic Framework for Time Series Forecasting

- **Authors:** Sarkar Snigdha Sarathi Das et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2026; arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14389
- **Method summary:** Decomposes forecasts into macro and micro temporal reasoning, contextual retrieval, and synthesis by specialized agents.
- **Datasets:** Zillow metrics and volatile stock-market equities after LLM knowledge cutoffs.
- **Key reported result:** Reportedly matches or outperforms TSFMs and LLM baselines on post-cutoff sets.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** No calibrated uncertainty, transaction costs, or controlled contamination audit in the abstract.
- **Sub-topic:** agentic contextual forecasting
