# Temporal Fusion Transformers for Interpretable Multi-horizon Time Series Forecasting

- **Authors:** Bryan Lim et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2021; International Journal of Forecasting / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09363
- **Method summary:** Combines recurrent local processing, variable selection, gating, and interpretable attention.
- **Datasets:** Retail, electricity, and traffic-style datasets.
- **Key reported result:** Outperformed strong baselines while providing variable-selection and attention views.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Authors caution that attention/importance is not causal explanation; no live news or regime-shift validation.
- **Sub-topic:** interpretable forecasting
