# iTransformer: Inverted Transformers Are Effective for Time Series Forecasting

- **Authors:** Yong Liu et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2024; ICLR / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06625
- **Method summary:** Embeds each variate as a token and applies attention across variates.
- **Datasets:** Seven real-world long-term forecasting datasets.
- **Key reported result:** Reports state-of-the-art results and improved multivariate representation quality.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** No uncertainty, evidence provenance, or event-aware finance tests.
- **Sub-topic:** backbone forecasting
