# Bridging the Last Mile of Time Series Forecasting with LLM Agents

- **Authors:** Yuhua Liao et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2026; arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02497
- **Method summary:** Adds retrieval, constrained forecast revision, long-horizon decomposition, and memory reflection on a forecasting backbone.
- **Datasets:** Real-world business case studies.
- **Key reported result:** Shows case-study evidence that agents can turn statistical forecasts into business-ready revisions.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Case studies rather than a standardized contamination-resistant benchmark.
- **Sub-topic:** agentic contextual forecasting
