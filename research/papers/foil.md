# Time-Series Forecasting for Out-of-Distribution Generalization Using Invariant Learning

- **Authors:** Haoxin Liu et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2024; ICML / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09130
- **Method summary:** Infers temporal environments and learns invariant representations with a surrogate loss for OOD forecasting.
- **Datasets:** Multiple public forecasting datasets with covariates.
- **Key reported result:** Reports gains up to 85% and uniform improvements across evaluated backbones.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Focuses on univariate targets with covariates; unobserved causes and missing environment labels remain challenges.
- **Sub-topic:** regime shift and OOD
