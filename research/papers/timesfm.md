# A Decoder-Only Foundation Model for Time-Series Forecasting

- **Authors:** Google Research authors
- **Year / venue:** 2023; arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10688
- **Method summary:** Pretrains a patched decoder-only Transformer on a large real and synthetic time-series corpus.
- **Datasets:** Large internal corpus and public benchmark datasets.
- **Key reported result:** Reports zero-shot performance close to supervised state of the art.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** External users cannot fully audit training-data overlap; finance robustness is not the focus.
- **Sub-topic:** probabilistic TSFM
