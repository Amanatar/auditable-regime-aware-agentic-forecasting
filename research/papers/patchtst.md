# A Time Series is Worth 64 Words: Long-term Forecasting with Transformers

- **Authors:** Yuqi Nie et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2023; ICLR / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14730
- **Method summary:** Segments histories into patches and uses channel-independent Transformer encoding.
- **Datasets:** ETT, electricity, traffic, weather, and exchange rate.
- **Key reported result:** Improves long-term accuracy versus Transformer baselines while reducing attention cost.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Channel independence limits explicit cross-series interaction; no news or regime-shift setting.
- **Sub-topic:** backbone forecasting
