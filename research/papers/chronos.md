# Chronos: Learning the Language of Time Series

- **Authors:** Abdul Fatir Ansari et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2024; TMLR / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07815
- **Method summary:** Scales, quantizes, and tokenizes values, then trains T5-style language models for probabilistic forecasting.
- **Datasets:** 42 public datasets plus Gaussian-process synthetic data.
- **Key reported result:** Outperforms methods on seen datasets and is competitive or occasionally superior zero-shot on unseen datasets.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Benchmark overlap and no finance-specific economic evaluation complicate deployment claims.
- **Sub-topic:** probabilistic TSFM
