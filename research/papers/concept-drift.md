# Handling Concept Drift in Global Time Series

- **Authors:** Ziyi Liu et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2023; arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.01512
- **Method summary:** Studies transfer-learning and concept-drift handling for global forecasting models as distributions change.
- **Datasets:** Four global time-series datasets plus statistical and LightGBM baselines.
- **Key reported result:** Shows drift-aware transfer can improve forecasting under changing distributions.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Not financial or agentic; authors frame global-forecasting drift handling as underdeveloped.
- **Sub-topic:** regime shift and OOD
