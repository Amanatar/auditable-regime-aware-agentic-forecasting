# Are Transformers Effective for Time Series Forecasting?

- **Authors:** Ailing Zeng et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2023; AAAI / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13504
- **Method summary:** Uses simple linear and decomposed-linear mappings as strong long-horizon baselines.
- **Datasets:** Nine real-life datasets: ETT, traffic, electricity, exchange rate, weather, ILI.
- **Key reported result:** LTSF-Linear often outperformed sophisticated Transformers.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Result is strongest for long-horizon benchmark settings; no multimodal evidence or probabilistic calibration.
- **Sub-topic:** backbone forecasting
