# Unified Training of Universal Time Series Forecasting Transformers

- **Authors:** Gerald Woo et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2024; ICML / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02592
- **Method summary:** Uses masked encoder pretraining, flexible patch sizes, and mixture distributions for universal forecasting.
- **Datasets:** LOTSA: over 27B observations across nine domains.
- **Key reported result:** Competitive or superior zero-shot performance versus full-shot models.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Financial regime shifts and post-deployment drift are not tested.
- **Sub-topic:** probabilistic TSFM
