# N-HiTS: Neural Hierarchical Interpolation for Time Series Forecasting

- **Authors:** Cristian Challu et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2022; AAAI / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12886
- **Method summary:** Uses hierarchical interpolation and multi-rate sampling for long-horizon forecasts.
- **Datasets:** Large-scale long-horizon benchmark datasets.
- **Key reported result:** Reports almost 20% average improvement over recent Transformers and up to 50x lower computation.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** Assumes smooth multi-scale structure; no textual evidence or abrupt financial shocks.
- **Sub-topic:** efficient backbones
