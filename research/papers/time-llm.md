# Time-LLM: Time Series Forecasting by Reprogramming Large Language Models

- **Authors:** Ming Jin et al.
- **Year / venue:** 2023; ICLR / arXiv
- **URL / DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01728
- **Method summary:** Reprograms numerical patches into textual prototypes and feeds them to a frozen LLM.
- **Datasets:** ETT, electricity, traffic, weather, and exchange-rate benchmarks.
- **Key reported result:** Reports gains over prior methods on several benchmarks without updating the LLM.
- **Author-stated limitation or scope boundary:** No live news grounding, regime-shift, or financial decision-utility evaluation.
- **Sub-topic:** LLM-based time-series models
